    Resume, interviews, and career guidance

    A short checklist for each stage of the job search.

    Resume

    Make it easy to scan

    • Keep it to one page as a fresher.
    • Lead each project with what you built and the result.
    • List the stack you used, not every tool you have seen.
    • Link a live demo and your repository.
    Interview

    Practice out loud

    • Revise core Java, SQL, and web fundamentals.
    • Solve a few data-structure problems each week.
    • Prepare a two-minute walkthrough of your best project.
    • Explain trade-offs, not only answers.
    Career

    Choose a direction

    • Pick one role to aim at first, such as full stack or data.
    • Build two or three projects that fit that role.
    • Start applying before you feel completely ready.
    • Track applications in a simple sheet.
